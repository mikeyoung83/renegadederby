We created a non-profit organization, based on democratic principles, operating as part of the larger derby community. We are proud to have become a full member league of the Women's Flat Track Derby Association in March of 2014, which means that we joined the ranks of 470 women-led, skater owned and operated leagues worldwide that have united to lead the sport of women's flat track roller derby.
